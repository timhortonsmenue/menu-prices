How to Customize Your Order at Tim Hortons

  • Coffee your way: ask for a Double-Double (two creams, two sugars), a Triple-Triple, or any mix of cream, milk, sugar or sweetener.
  • Change the milk in lattes and iced drinks; plant-based options are available at many locations.
  • Add an espresso shot to a coffee or Iced Capp for an extra boost.
  • Swap the bread on breakfast sandwiches: English muffin, biscuit or bagel.
  • Pick your toppings for bagels: butter, plain or flavoured cream cheese.
  • Order ahead in the Tim Hortons app to save your favourite custom order and collect Tims Rewards points.

Tim Hortons Nutrition & Allergen Information

Calories and allergens change with size, bread and add-ons, so the most accurate source is the nutrition and allergen information in the Tim Hortons app and on the official Tim Hortons website. About Tim Hortons Canada

Tim Hortons was founded in 1964 in Hamilton, Ontario, by hockey player Tim Horton and grew into Canada's best-known coffee and donut chain. Today it is part of Restaurant Brands International and has more than 6,000 restaurants in Canada, the United States and around the world. For many Canadians, a morning Tims run is part of daily life.

Conclusion

The Tim Hortons menu keeps the classics that made it famous, such as the Original Blend, Double-Double, Timbits and the Apple Fritter, while adding cold brew, Loaded Bowls and new seasonal drinks every year. Use this page to compare sizes and prices before you order, and remember that prices can differ slightly from one restaurant to the next.

Tim Hortons Menu FAQ's

How much is a coffee at Tim Hortons in Canada?

An Original Blend coffee costs about $1.59 for a Small, $1.83 for a Medium and $2.07 for a Large. Dark Roast is the same price.

How much is a dozen donuts at Tim Hortons?

A box of 12 assorted donuts costs about $14.49, which works out to roughly $1.21 per donut. A box of six is about $7.99.

How much is a 50 pack of Timbits?

A 50 pack of assorted Timbits costs about $9.99. The 20 pack is about $5.19 and the 10 pack about $3.19.

How much is an Iced Capp?

An Original Iced Capp is about $3.29 Small, $3.79 Medium and $4.29 Large. Flavoured Iced Capps start at about $3.99.

Does Tim Hortons serve breakfast all day?

Yes. Most Tim Hortons restaurants in Canada serve the breakfast menu all day, although some locations may differ.

How much is Take Twelve at Tim Hortons?

A Take Twelve box of coffee, which serves 12, costs about $19.99. It's a popular choice for office meetings and family gatherings.

Are Tim Hortons menu prices the same across Canada?

No. Most restaurants are franchises, so prices can change by province, city and store type, such as airports or highway stops. Sales tax is added at the till.

What does Double-Double mean?

A Double-Double is a Tim Hortons coffee with two creams and two sugars. You can also order a Triple-Triple or any mix you like.
